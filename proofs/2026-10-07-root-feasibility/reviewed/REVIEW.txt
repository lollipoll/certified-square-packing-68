Mathematical audit

The contraction proof correctly bounds the infinity-norm derivative of
z -> z - R F(z) on the box and shows that its image lies strictly inside.
The inverse defect implies that R and the Jacobian are nonsingular. Thus the
unique fixed point is the unique zero of F in the box. The quadratic Hessian
bound and all arithmetic used for acceptance are exact rationals.

Both new implementations reconstruct the geometry from the selected system.
The exact unit/orthogonality identities give 68 unit-square frames. Every
unordered pair is covered once, without neighbor culling: four corners of one
square are proved to lie beyond a supporting edge of the other. Convexity
then proves disjoint interiors. All four corners against all four walls give
1,088 containment predicates. Boundary contact is allowed.

Results agree:
  2,278 unordered pairs; 1,088 corner/wall predicates; 68 unit frames;
  255 exact-zero predicates and 9,945 strictly positive predicates;
  437 proposed polynomial equations implied by the 153 selected equations.

The zero identities are checked coefficient by coefficient as constant
rational linear combinations, rather than accepted numerical residuals.
They use at most eight rows, with coefficients -1,-1/2,1/2,1,3/2. There are
no variable denominators in these identities. The positive branches use
sound outward enclosures of the original root box. The smallest certified
positive predicate margin is

The two implementations use different arithmetic paths: expanded Fraction
