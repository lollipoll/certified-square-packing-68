Analytic contact reduction and proof scopes

The square labels are 0,...,67 in the published certificate's ordering.
The final common-orientation groups, verified as feasible in the rational
certificate after unconstrained numerical fitting, are:

 A = {8,14,17,21,23,28,30,33,36,38,42,45,47,52,55}
 B = {20,25,29,31,35,37,39,44,46,50,53,59}
 C = {12}, D = {13}, E = {16}, F = {22}, G = {34}, H = {43}.

A and B have distinct angles. The reduction imposes these within-group
equalities as the definition of a restricted geometric family; it does not
assert that every neighboring optimal packing must have them. Neither global
reflection nor half-turn symmetry is imposed or supported by the coordinates.

Let X_i=(x_i,y_i), u_i=(c_i,s_i), v_i=(-s_i,c_i), and
  P_i(a,b) = X_i + (a u_i + b v_i)/2,   a,b in {-1,+1}.
For each group j impose c_j^2+s_j^2=1. Other orientations are exact rational
unit directions defined by the fixed half-angle parameters in system.json.
For an edge of square k with outward normal n=epsilon*u_k or epsilon*v_k,
a contact with vertex P_i(a,b) has equation
  n dot (P_i(a,b)-X_k) - 1/2 = 0.
The corresponding supporting half-plane requires the expression >= 0.
For a wall with coordinate d and sign epsilon, the equation is
  epsilon*P_i(a,b)_d - L/2 = 0,
and feasibility requires this expression <= 0.
Every polynomial is degree at most two, with rational coefficients.

The fully specified exact polynomial system is results/reduced/system.json:
153 variables ordered x[68], y[68], L, c_A,s_A,...,c_H,s_H. It preserves all
proposed equations separately from the chosen independent basis. Twenty-two
rational center-coordinate gauges fix sliding freedoms. They are explicitly
listed as gauges, not presented as physical contacts.

For fixed eight orientations, the 137 selected center/side rows have the form
  A(t) z + b(t) = 0,
where z=(x[68],y[68],L), c_j=(1-t_j^2)/(1+t_j^2), and
s_j=2*t_j/(1+t_j^2). The file's linear_rows identify these rows unambiguously.
An exact preconditioner check bounds the inverse defect below 8.45e-10 throughout the radius-10^-11 box (elimination_verification.json), so this elimination is nonsingular there. In that neighborhood, z=-A(t)^(-1)b(t). Substitution into the
remaining eight contact rows gives
  R_k(t) = B_k(t)[-A(t)^(-1)b(t)] + d_k(t) = 0,  k=1,...,8.
These are eight rational equations in eight variables. Multiplying their
numerators by the nonzero denominators gives an exact algebraic description.
The determinant/inverse formula, source rows, and rational coefficients are
provided instead of an enormous expanded eliminant. No assertion of a
minimal polynomial's degree is made.

The 50-, 100-, and 180-digit solves and all omitted-equation residuals are

Exact root enclosure

Let F be the chosen square polynomial system, x the rational 120-place center,
R a rationalized approximate inverse, and r=10^-100. The verifier evaluates,
using Fraction throughout,
  beta = ||R F(x)||_infinity,
  delta = ||I - R J(x)||_infinity,
  M = ||R||_infinity,
  H >= max_i sum_{j,k} |d^2 F_i/(dx_j dx_k)|.
All second derivatives are constant. It checks
  q = delta + M H r < 1,
  beta + q r < r.
The map y -> y-R F(y) is then a contraction from the box into itself. Because
R J is invertible throughout it, its unique fixed point is a unique zero of F.
Measured rigorous bounds are approximately beta=4.97542e-121 and
delta=3.16352e-11. The exact fractions, center, inverse, and box are saved in
root_witness.json and root_verification.json. The side interval is the center's
L coordinate plus/minus 10^-100. This does not certify every inactive packing
inequality at the unrounded root. It also does not imply optimality.

Restricted local lower bound

Hold the first 137 rows and eight circles equal to zero. Orient each of the
last eight rows so that g_k>=0 is feasible (the bottom-wall row changes sign).
For quadratic F the exact identity
  F(y)-F(y*) = J((y+y*)/2)(y-y*)
holds. If both points are in the radius-rho box, an exact inverse estimate is
  ||J(midpoint)^(-1)-R|| <= delta_rho/(1-delta_rho) * M,
  delta_rho = delta + M H rho.
At rho=10^-11 the last eight sign-corrected entries in row L of the inverse
are all positive: the smallest center value is 1.604038e-6 and the uniform
uncertainty is less than 1.530562e-6. Therefore
  L(y)-L(y*) = sum_k positive_coefficient_k * g_k(y) >= 0.
Equality forces all eight gaps to zero and hence y=y*, by invertibility.
This proves a unique minimum of the specified contact relaxation in that box,
and a lower bound for actual packings satisfying its held equalities.
The equality constraints, 22 gauges, common angles, small box, and fixed other
orientations are essential restrictions. Attainment as an exact algebraic
68-square packing is not asserted, and opening the held contacts is unproved.

Fixed-orientation minimum enclosure

For the final rational orientation parameters, choose one directed supporting
edge for each of the 2,278 pairs. Require all four vertices of the other square
to lie in its outer half-plane; add every vertex/wall inequality. This produces
10,200 affine inequalities in the 136 center coordinates and L, of the form
  a_r dot z + b_r >= 0.
The exact nonnegative dual weights satisfy
  sum_r lambda_r a_r = e_L.
Consequently every packing in this precisely specified convex family has
  L >= -sum_r lambda_r b_r.
Only 32 nonzero weights are needed. The final rational certificate satisfies
all 10,200 inequalities, so the actual family minimum lies between this exact
lower bound and its certified side. Their difference is less than 4.59e-61.
This global-within-the-family statement allows all translations but fixes all
angles and the pairwise supporting-edge choices. It excludes no packing using
different orientations or different separating choices.

Exact finite export and independent feasibility

Centers are rounded to denominator 10^80 and multiplied by 1+10^-70.
Half-angle parameters are rounded to denominator 10^80. These rational t's
produce exactly orthogonal, exactly unit-length directions. The enclosing side
is rounded upward to 60 decimals after reserving 10^-68 extra space. The
resulting geometry is verified directly, so the safety of these heuristic
rounding choices is not assumed. Both exact verifiers reject any negative gap.
All 68 squares and all 2,278 pairs pass. The certified side is approximately
4.58039e-61 above the enclosed contact limit.

Movements and contact changes

For a proposed single-square translation delta*d with fixed angles, every
chosen half-plane inequality is affine in delta. Intersecting their exact
rational bounds gives a certified interval for the entire motion, including
all intermediate positions. results/exact_motions.json records the intervals
for 21 squares. The square-54 example in moved54_exact.json is separately
verified. These are finite motions, not just Jacobian null vectors.

The main contact switch is quantified by the two possible normals of the
13/16 pair. At the earlier branch the edge owned by 13 is active and the
alternative has about -2.77e-8 projected clearance. At the improved branch the
edge owned by 16 is active and the old axis has about -3.40e-9 clearance. A
negative gap on one axis alone does not mean overlap: SAT needs one separating
axis. Square 13 also reaches square 22 on the improved branch. The saved
continuations explicitly change these supporting choices instead of keeping
the earlier contact system fixed.
