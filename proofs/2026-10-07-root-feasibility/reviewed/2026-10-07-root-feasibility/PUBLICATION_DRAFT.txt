UNPUBLISHED DRAFT — exact feasibility of the selected n=68 contact root

Seth Rehwaldt, with OpenAI Codex assistance, has completed the missing
all-inequality certificate for the selected algebraic contact root accompanying
certified-square-packing-68 v1.1.0. If L* is the side coordinate of that system's
uniquely enclosed root, the construction proves s(68)<=L*.

Two standard-library arithmetic implementations check all 2,278 pairs, all
1,088 corner/wall predicates and all 68 unit frames. Of 10,200 inequality
predicates, 9,945 have strict rational interval bounds and 255 follow from
exact contact identities. All 437 descriptive equations are verified rational
linear combinations of the 153 selected equations. Twenty rejection controls
pass. The system, gauges, orientations and original root enclosure are unchanged.

The gain over the published rational side is approximately 4.58039e-61;
mathematical completeness is the contribution. The released rational certificate
and its bound remain valid. This is no claim of unrestricted optimality, a new
arrangement, worldwide priority, or independent human review.
