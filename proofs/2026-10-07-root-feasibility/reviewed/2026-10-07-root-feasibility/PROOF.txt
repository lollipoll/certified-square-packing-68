PROOF OF THE ENCLOSURE. The released exact contraction verifier is replayed.
For rational center q, rational matrix R and r=10^-100 it bounds
beta=||R F(q)||_infinity, delta=||I-R J(q)||_infinity, M=||R||_infinity,
and the constant quadratic Hessian norm H. It proves
qcon=delta+M H r<1 and beta+qcon*r<r.
Thus z -> z-R F(z) maps the closed box into itself contractively. Its fixed
point is a zero because R J is nonsingular (defect<1), hence R is nonsingular.
Every zero is a fixed point, proving uniqueness. Reproduced beta is about
4.97542e-121 and delta about 3.16352e-11; acceptance uses exact fractions.
The original 137-variable elimination also reproduces, with inverse defect
bound below 8.445e-10 in its radius-10^-11 box. It is not needed to expand
any eliminant or to prove the new identities. The root box has not been shrunk.

PROOF OF THE MISSING INEQUALITIES. For each unordered pair {i,j}, the certificate
selects an owner k in {i,j} and a signed normal n=epsilon*u_k or epsilon*v_k.
For all four corners P of the other square it proves
                 g(P)=n dot (P-X_k)-1/2 >= 0.
By convexity every point of that other square is in the outer closed half-plane.
The owner's interior satisfies n dot (P-X_k)<1/2, so the interiors are disjoint.
This covers each of the 2,278 pairs exactly once, with no broad-phase omissions.
Axes from either owner are allowed: 866 selected edges belong to the larger
index. For example pair (13,16) uses square 16's +v edge, with one exact contact.
One failed alternative axis never determines the outcome.

For each of four corners of every square, the four containment predicates are
L/2-epsilon*P_d>=0, d in {x,y}, epsilon in {-1,+1}. These 1,088 predicates
contain the whole convex square. There are 9,112 pair predicates, hence 10,200
geometric inequality predicates in total.
